# Content Language

## Token and Treasury Language

- Focus on use, participation, governance, coordination, and product behavior.
- Do not frame tokens as investments or promise returns/upside/profit.
- Avoid equity metaphors (shares/dividends/profit sharing) and yield terms (APY/APR/interest).
- Include risk caveats when discussing mechanisms tied to value transfer.

## Platform Naming

- Use "Farcaster" in user-facing copy.
- Avoid "Warpcast" except where strictly required for technical specificity.

## Escalation

Escalate expected returns, trading advice, legal claims, and borderline financial phrasing.
